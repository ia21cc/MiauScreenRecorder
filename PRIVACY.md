# Privacy Policy – Miau Screen Recorder

_Last updated: November 2026_

Miau Screen Recorder runs entirely on your computer.

- **No data collection.** The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell personal information. It has no accounts, analytics or advertising.
- **No network use.** The app does not send your recordings, screen content, camera images or audio anywhere.
- **Screen, audio and camera.** The screen, desktop audio, microphone and camera are accessed only to create the recordings or photos you ask for. Files are saved to the folder you choose (by default your Videos/Pictures folder) and never leave your device unless you move them.
- **Local files.** Your preferences are stored locally in `%APPDATA%\MiauScreenRecorder`, together with a small `log.txt` used only for troubleshooting. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Third parties.** The app uses FFmpeg (LGPL) for encoding. See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

Contact: open an issue in the project's GitHub repository or visit https://ia21.cc
